Why choose Verdi?

Discover the story behind three quiet pool villas in Thalang.

Private pool living

Every villa has its own pool, garden and entrance. No shared facilities and no neighbours looking in.

Single-storey design

Every room sits on one level and opens straight onto the terrace and pool, under high ceilings with wide glass openings.

Close to everything

1.6 km to Robinson Lifestyle, 9 km to Bang Tao beach and about twenty minutes to the airport.

Verdi is three single-storey villas by Opera Estate in Si Sunthon, Thalang, the green middle of Phuket. Each has its own pool and garden, open rooms under high ceilings, large glass openings and finishes in wood and natural stone.

Investment

Income, payments and payback

Rental programme

6%a year, projected

The management company rents out your villa and looks after marketing, bookings, guests and upkeep. The 6% is a projection, not a guarantee.

Instalment plan

The price is paid in stages as the villa is built.

  • 30%Deposit and contract signing, 30 days after booking
  • 65%During construction: foundation, structure and pool, roof, then tiling, mechanical and electrical
  • 5%Handover on completion

Payback period

10years

The point at which the villa could have paid for itself. It is a forecast from the rental programme figures, not a promise.

Can foreign buyers own a villa?
Yes. The villas are sold freehold, including to foreign buyers. We recommend an independent lawyer in Thailand checks the title and contract before you sign.
How does the rental programme work?
It is optional. The villa is let to holiday guests, with marketing, bookings, guest services and maintenance handled for you. The return is projected at 6% a year; it is a projection, not a guarantee.
Who is building Verdi?
Verdi Pool Villas is developed by Opera Estate. We post dated photos from site on the Progress page.
How are payments made?
30% at contract signing, 65% in instalments during construction and the final 5% at transfer.
Can I visit before I decide?
Yes. Viewings run every day on site in Thalang, or by video call if you are abroad.
