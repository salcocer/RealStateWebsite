Why choose Verdi?

Discover the story behind three quiet pool villas in Thalang.

Private pool living

Every villa has its own pool, garden and entrance. No shared facilities and no neighbours looking in.

Single-storey design

Every room sits on one level and opens straight onto the terrace, under timber ceilings over four metres high.

Close to everything

Five minutes to Robinson Lifestyle, 9 km to Bang Tao beach and about twenty minutes to the airport.

Verdi is three single-storey villas on one quiet plot near Robinson Lifestyle in Thalang, the green middle of Phuket. They are built the Tropical Modern way, with white render, hipped shingle roofs, teak screens and natural stone, and deep eaves that keep the rooms cool.

Can foreign buyers own a villa?
Yes, through a registered 30-year lease of the land with ownership of the villa itself. Thai buyers and Thai companies can hold the land freehold. We do not use nominee structures.
How does the rental programme work?
It is optional. The villa is let to holiday guests and managed for you. The listing advertises 7% a year for the first two years; who pays it and what follows are set out in the contract.
When will the villas be finished?
Handover is planned for May 2027. We post dated photos from site every month on the Progress page.
How are payments made?
A reservation deposit, then instalments tied to construction milestones, with the balance at handover after your inspection.
Can I visit before I decide?
Yes. Viewings run every day on site in Thalang, or by video call if you are abroad.
