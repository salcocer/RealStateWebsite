Verdi

Designed for Modern Living

a boutique collection

Verdi is three single-storey villas on one quiet plot near Robinson Lifestyle in Thalang, the green middle of Phuket. Each has its own pool, its own garden and its own entrance.

The houses are built in the Tropical Modern way: white render, hipped shingle roofs, teak screens and natural stone, with high ceilings and deep eaves that keep the rooms cool. Bang Tao beach is 9 km away, the airport about twenty minutes, and the island's international schools are close by.
