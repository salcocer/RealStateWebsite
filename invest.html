Investment and ownership

Income, payments and title

How the rental programme works, how payments are staged, and how ownership is held for Thai and foreign buyers.

Rental programme

Income from the first year, with the terms in plain sight

Owners who are not living at Verdi full time can join an optional managed rental programme. The villa is let to holiday guests, cleaned and maintained between stays, and the owner keeps the right to use it for agreed weeks each year.

The programme is advertised with a return of 7% a year for the first two years after handover. We show the figure with its terms rather than as a headline. Ask us for the draft programme agreement.

  • Paid byTo be confirmed in contract
  • After year 2Share of net rental income, to be confirmed
  • Owner useAgreed weeks each year

Payment plan

Paid in stages, tied to the build

  1. Reservation

    A refundable deposit holds the villa while contracts are prepared and reviewed by your lawyer.

  2. Sale agreement

    The first instalment on signing. The full schedule is set out in the contract.

  3. Construction milestones

    Instalments as the structure, roof and finishes are completed, each confirmed with dated photos.

  4. Handover, May 2027

    The balance on completion, after your inspection, with title or lease registered at the Land Office.

Ownership

How title is held under Thai law

Freehold for Thai buyers

Thai nationals and Thai-majority companies can hold the land and villa freehold, registered on a Chanote title deed.

Registered leasehold

Foreigners cannot own land in Thailand. The usual route is a 30-year lease of the land, registered at the Land Office, with ownership of the villa itself.

No nominee structures

We do not offer companies with nominee Thai shareholders. They are not legal and can leave owners without protection.

The 49% foreign quota often mentioned applies to condominium units, not to villas on their own land. This page is general information, not legal advice. Please appoint an independent lawyer in Thailand before you sign.
