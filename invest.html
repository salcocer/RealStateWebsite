Investment and ownership

Income, payments and title

How the rental programme works, how payments are staged, and how freehold ownership works, including for foreign buyers.

Rental programme

Rental income, with the terms in plain sight

Owners who are not living at Verdi full time can join an optional managed rental programme. The villa is marketed and let to holiday guests, with bookings, guest services and maintenance handled for the owner.

The return is projected at 6% a year. It is a projection, not a guarantee, and actual income depends on bookings. Ask us for the programme terms.

The terms

  • Projected return6% a year
  • GuaranteedNo, it is a projection
  • ServicesMarketing, bookings, guest services, maintenance
  • Management companyTo be confirmed
  • FeesTo be confirmed
  • Owner useTo be confirmed

Payment plan

Paid in three stages, tied to the build

  1. 30% at contract

    The first payment when you sign the sale agreement, after your lawyer has reviewed it.

  2. 65% during construction

    Paid in instalments as the build progresses, each stage shown with dated photos from site.

  3. 5% at transfer

    The balance when ownership is transferred to you at the Land Office.

Ownership

Freehold ownership

Freehold title

Each villa is sold freehold with its own plot, registered at the Land Office.

Open to foreign buyers

Freehold is offered to foreign buyers as well as Thai buyers. Your lawyer will confirm how title is registered for your purchase.

Independent advice

We recommend appointing an independent lawyer in Thailand to check the title and contract before you sign.

This page is general information, not legal advice. Please appoint an independent lawyer in Thailand before you sign.
