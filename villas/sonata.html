The Villa

A House That Opens to the Garden

Every room on one level, every room a step from the water.

Sonata has the largest plot and the widest garden. Its living room runs the full depth of the house under a high timber ceiling, with glass on both sides so the breeze moves straight through.

The plan is simple. Bedrooms sit at either end, each with an en-suite bathroom and walk-in closet; the living room, dining area and kitchen share one tall space in the middle under a timber ceiling. Folding glass doors open the whole room to the terrace.

Materials are chosen for the climate: white render and hipped shingle roofs outside, teak, natural stone and terrazzo within. Deep eaves shade the glass, and cross-ventilation keeps the house comfortable before the air conditioning goes on.
